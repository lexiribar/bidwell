Ministries

Ways to serve, study, and belong

Inside these walls and well beyond them.

At the church

  • Worship & Celebration

    Praise and worship is a cornerstone of this church. Our music ministry is the Voices of Judah Choir, the Praise and Worship Team, and the mime ministry “Just One Breath of Life.”

  • Christian Education

    Wednesday evenings at 7:00 p.m., a group of adults meets on Zoom to work through the Bible book by book. Open to anyone — call the church office for the link.

  • Sunday School

    Children begin the morning in worship and go downstairs together before the message. Men’s and women’s Bible studies meet Sundays at 10:00 a.m.

  • Youth Ministry

    Young people study, worship, and fellowship together — and have a great deal of fun. Skating, bowling, movies, and quiet moments of reflection.

  • Mission & Evangelism

    Reaching beyond the walls of this church to share the Gospel and serve people in need.

  • Hospitality

    Tending to the house of God, its members, and its guests. You will meet this ministry at the door on Sunday morning.

  • Audio-Visual

    Capturing worship so it can be shared with members who are unable to attend in person.

In the community

Bidwell has always understood the church as something that happens in the neighborhood, not only in the sanctuary.

  • Pittsburgh Interfaith Impact Network (PIIN) — a member congregation, joining people of faith across Southwestern Pennsylvania to act on issues of justice and fairness.
  • Presbyterian Women — nurturing faith through prayer and Bible study, supporting mission worldwide, and working for justice and peace.
  • Presbyterian Men — leading men into a vital relationship with Christ and into service at home, at church, at work, and in the world.
  • Boy Scout Troop #281 and Cub Scout Pack — sponsored by Bidwell, meeting weekly and open to boys from anywhere in the city.
  • Girl Scouts — building leadership, character, and self-worth in an accepting and nurturing environment.
  • Synod of Blantyre, Malawi — our partnership with the Church of Central Africa Presbyterian.
  • Camp Bidwell and the summer lunch program — for children in the Manchester neighborhood. (Needs confirming.)
