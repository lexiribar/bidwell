Everything you need to know before your first Sunday at Bidwell.

Your first Sunday

Worship begins at 11:00 a.m. every Sunday. If you would like to start the morning with Bible study, classes for men, women, and youth meet at 10:00 a.m.

Come as you are. Members of our hospitality ministry will be at the door to welcome you, help you find a seat, and answer any questions. If you are visiting with children, they are welcome in worship, and Sunday school meets beforehand at 10:00 a.m.
