Ministries

Ways to serve, study, and belong

Every ministry at Bidwell exists so that people can grow in Christ and use the gifts God has given them.

  • Mission & Evangelism

    This ministry has as its mission the task of reaching beyond the walls of Bidwell Presbyterian Church and sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ as set forth in Scripture. We depend on the Spirit of God, who draws us and others to Christ, working through us as we reach out and serve people in need.

  • Hospitality

    The hospitality ministry tends to the needs of the house of God, its members, and its guests. Members of this ministry can be seen as you enter the church on Sunday for worship and on special occasions, greeting visitors and serving those who enter the house of the Lord. Our focus is on making each experience at Bidwell one that leaves a lasting impression that you have been with people who love the Lord.

  • Youth Ministry

    Our youth ministry is full of energy and excitement as we help young people discover the joy that only the Lord can bring to their lives. Youth learn about the Lord, study the Bible, worship, and fellowship together — and have a great deal of fun. The ministry sponsors a wide range of activities: skating, bowling, movies, spirit-filled parties, quiet moments of reflection, and much more.

  • Worship & Celebration

    This ministry focuses on lifting up praise and worship to God in His house as one of the cornerstones of our church. Our music ministry consists of the Voices of Judah Choir, the Praise and Worship Team, and the mime ministry known as “Just One Breath of Life.” Through these various forms, we give honor and glory to God through spirit-led praise and worship.

  • Audio-Visual

    The Audio-Visual Ministry is in place to capture the worship experience of our congregation so it can be shared with those who are infirm or geographically prohibited from attending worship. This ministry extends our worship beyond the walls of the sanctuary. (Previously, recordings of Sunday services were available on CD — needs confirming, and worth replacing with a livestream or podcast link if one exists.)

  • Christian Education

    One of the cornerstones of Bidwell Church is our focus on strengthening our relationship with Christ through knowledge of Him. On Wednesday evenings at 7:00 p.m., a group of adults meets on Zoom to explore the Bible book by book, chapter by chapter, and verse by verse. These classes are open to anyone who wants to read and understand God’s word through prayer and fellowship. In order to walk as He did, you must first read and understand His word.

    Call the church office for the Zoom link.

  • Sunday School

    Children begin the morning in worship with their families. Partway through the service, before the message, they go downstairs together for Sunday school and rejoin the congregation afterward.

    Men’s and women’s Bible studies meet every Sunday morning at 10:00 a.m., before the worship service.

Interested in joining a ministry?

Call the church office at 412-231-4663 or speak with any member of the hospitality ministry on a Sunday morning — they will point you to the right person.
