Leadership

Rev. Dr. B. DeNeice Welch

Senior Pastor. Biography carried over from the previous site — needs confirming and updating.

A passion for prayer, praise, mission, and evangelism

Rev. B. DeNeice Welch was born in West Virginia and grew up in New Jersey. She received a BA in business administration from Robert Morris College and a BS in Community Ministry from Geneva College. She earned her Master of Divinity from Pittsburgh Theological Seminary in 2004. She was first ordained as a Presbyterian minister and installed as Associate Pastor for Outreach at Shadyside Presbyterian Church in 2005. She accepted a call and was installed as Pastor of Bidwell Presbyterian Church in May 2007.

During her time at Shadyside Church, she coordinated a number of mission trips, including outreach to the Gulf Coast in the wake of Hurricane Katrina and to Presbyterian mission partners in Malawi, Africa. During her time as Evangelism Ministry Chair of Bidwell Church, she launched a summer lunch and camp program for children in the Manchester neighborhood. Her passion for teaching and studying God’s word led her to create a women’s Bible study group as a partnership between Bidwell and Shadyside churches, focused on the “Women at the Cross.”

As a seminary student, DeNeice served a yearlong internship at Valley View Presbyterian Church in Stanton Heights, where she was student pastor, led worship, organized a weekly Bible study, communicants and discipleship groups, and partnered with community development groups in Garfield. As a Resources Associate at Shady Lane School, she worked with women participating in a welfare-to-work program to train high-quality child care workers.

“Mission is an area where we, as servants of God, are afforded the opportunity to demonstrate God’s love for people who have difficulty seeing that love in their lives. In God’s economy, those who give of themselves receive much more than they can imagine. We always give from a position of gratitude for the grace that God has demonstrated in our lives, and seek only to pass along the joy of knowing that God is indeed active in the world today.” Rev. Dr. B. DeNeice Welch

Making disciples has always been DeNeice’s passion. She enjoys gardening, singing, and reading — especially the novels of J. California Cooper and the local writer Sharon Flake. She is married to the Rev. John C. Welch. She has four children — Aja, Jordan, Christopher, and Ian — and a grandchild, Ayanna, who are the joys in her life.
